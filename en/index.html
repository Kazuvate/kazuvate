Web design for SMEs in Switzerland

Who we are

kazuvate builds websites for small and medium-sized businesses in Switzerland. No generalists, no borrowed templates: every site is built by hand, for a business that lives on customers from its region.

That focus is deliberate. Serve one kind of client and you get good at it fast.

What drives us

Businesses that master their trade and still barely show up online: we kept running into that gap. The big agencies too expensive, the website builders too generic, nothing in between.

That is what we change. If you get up early and do clean work, you deserve a steady flow of enquiries.

Who we work with

Plumbing, electrical and heating, cleaning, landscaping, painting and plastering, transport, fiduciaries and medical practices: businesses that live on their local area. If you know your trade and want more people to find you, we are the right people.

We deliberately keep our client list short. Your project does not go to a junior team; you talk directly to the people who build it.

What we build for you

  • One-pager

    A single page with everything that matters: services, contact details and directions. For businesses that want to be found without maintaining a large website.

  • Multi-page website

    Five to eight pages with their own structure for services, team and references. The usual choice when several offers each need their own place.

  • Maintenance and updates

    You send us an email, we have the change live within 24 hours. Plus hosting, backups and an eye on loading speed.
